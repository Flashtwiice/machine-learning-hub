Customer Purchase Prediction

A Random Forest classifier trained with scikit-learn.

1. Problem Context

An online store wants to know, before a visitor leaves the site, whether they are likely to make a purchase. The data represent individual site visits, each described by the visitor's age, annual income, and time spent browsing the site. The purpose of applying a Random Forest is to combine these three variables and classify a visitor as Will Purchase or Will Not Purchase.
