4. The Three Iterations

In each iteration: (1) the Euclidean distance d = √((x−cx)² + (y−cy)²) from every record to the 3 centroids is computed, (2) each record joins the nearest centroid, and (3) each centroid moves to the mean of its records.

After three iterations the algorithm found three well-differentiated household profiles. The clusters differ mainly in how much energy is used (Cluster 1 vs. 2 vs. 3) and in when it is used (Cluster 2 concentrates use in the evening). A utility could use this to design time-of-use tariffs for Cluster 2 and efficiency programs for Cluster 3.
