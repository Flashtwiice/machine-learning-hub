Diabetes Risk Classification

A Binary Logistic Regression model trained with scikit-learn.

1. Problem Context

Clinics use blood glucose readings during routine checkups to flag patients who may need further diabetes screening. The data represent individual patients, each with a measured blood glucose level and a diagnosis. The purpose of applying Logistic Regression is to learn the relationship between glucose level and diabetes status, and use it to classify a new patient as Diabetic or Non-Diabetic based on a single glucose reading.
