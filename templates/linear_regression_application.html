Predicting Monthly Water Consumption

A Simple Linear Regression model trained with scikit-learn.

1. Problem Context

Water utility companies need to estimate how much water a household will consume in a month. The phenomenon analyzed here is the relationship between the number of people living in a household and its monthly water consumption. The data represent household-level observations collected over time. The purpose of applying Linear Regression is to learn this relationship automatically from data and use it to predict the expected monthly water consumption (in cubic meters) for a household with a given number of occupants, instead of relying on rough manual estimates.

Independent Variable (X)

Number of occupants

Unit: people per household

Dependent Variable (Y)

Monthly water consumption

Unit: cubic meters (m³) per month
