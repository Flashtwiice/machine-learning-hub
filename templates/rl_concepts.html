The Q-Learning Update Used Here

For every experience (s, a, r, s') the agent builds a learning target and moves Q(s,a) toward it:

target = r + γ · maxa' Q(s', a')    (if s' is the target: target = r)

predict() supplies Q(s', a') for the four actions and partial_fit() adjusts the weight of the pair (s, a) toward the target. Repeating this for hundreds of episodes makes the values spread backward from T to A.

Why a danger zone changes the problem: the shortest route from A to T crosses two D cells, but the safe route is only two moves longer. The agent can only discover this by experiencing the -25 penalties and comparing the returns, which is exactly what Reinforcement Learning is for.
